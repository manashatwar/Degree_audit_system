## Vision Statement

To develop an automated degree audit system for LNMIIT that enables students, advisors, and administrators to efficiently track academic progress, verify degree requirements, and ensure timely graduation by replacing manual audit processes with an intelligent, real-time tracking solution.

---

## Problem Statement

Currently, LNMIIT lacks an automated system for tracking student academic progress against degree requirements. Students and advisors manually verify course completion against catalog requirements, leading to:

- **Time-consuming manual verification** of degree requirements
- **Errors in requirement tracking** and course mapping
- **Delayed identification** of at-risk students
- **Inefficient graduation eligibility determination**
- **Difficulty managing** catalog year changes and transfer credits
- **Limited visibility** into academic progress for stakeholders

---

## Objectives

1. **Automate degree requirement tracking** for all LNMIIT programs
2. **Provide real-time academic progress visibility** to students and advisors
3. **Identify at-risk students** based on academic performance criteria
4. **Streamline transcript import** and course record management
5. **Support multiple catalog years** and program structures
6. **Generate comprehensive audit reports** for graduation eligibility
7. **Enable efficient academic advising** through progress analytics

---

## Project Scope

### In Scope:
- Student degree audit viewing and progress tracking
- Faculty advisor dashboards with at-risk student identification
- Registrar transcript import and catalog management
- Multi-catalog year support (2020-2026)
- Course completion tracking and requirement satisfaction
- Graduation eligibility determination
- Transfer credit and course substitution recording
- User authentication and role-based access control

### Out of Scope:
- Course registration system integration
- Grade submission and management
- Fee payment processing
- Hostel and library management
- Direct ERP system integration (Phase 1)
- Mobile native applications
- Real-time course enrollment tracking

---

## Stakeholders

### Primary Stakeholders:
1. **Students** - View their degree audit, track progress, understand remaining requirements
2. **Faculty Advisors** - Monitor advisee progress, identify at-risk students
3. **Registrar Office** - Manage transcripts, catalog requirements, graduation eligibility
4. **Academic Administrators (HOD, Dean)** - Access department analytics and reports

### Secondary Stakeholders:
5. **Admission Cell** - Record transfer credits and course substitutions
6. **System Administrators** - Manage user accounts and system configuration

---

## Success Criteria

- ✅ System successfully tracks degree progress for all active students
- ✅ Students can view their audit report within 2 seconds
- ✅ At-risk students identified automatically based on defined criteria
- ✅ Transcript import processes 100+ records in under 30 seconds
- ✅ 95%+ accuracy in requirement satisfaction calculation
- ✅ System supports all LNMIIT B.Tech programs and catalog years

## Technology Stack (Proposed)

- **Frontend**: React.js with modern UI components
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express
- **Data Storage**: localStorage (prototype) → Database (production)
- **Development Tools**: VS Code, Git, npm/yarn
